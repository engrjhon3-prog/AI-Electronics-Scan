Installing the APK

The app is distributed directly — not yet on the Play Store.

  1. On your Android phone, tap Download APK above.
  2. Open the downloaded ai-electronics-scanner.apk.
  3. If prompted, allow your browser to install unknown apps (Settings → Install unknown apps). This is normal for apps outside the Play Store.
  4. Tap Install, then open the app and scan your first component.

FAQ

Does it need internet?

No — identification, pinouts, wiring, and code all run on your phone. Internet is only needed to download the app and future updates.

Which boards are supported?

Arduino Uno and ESP32 today (Nano and ESP8266 wiring is largely identical). More boards are on the roadmap.

Which components can it recognise?

Resistors (with value decoding), plus labelled parts: NE555, L293D, DHT11, HC-SR04, HC-SR501 PIR, MPU-6050, SSD1306 OLED, HC-05 Bluetooth, SG90 servo, relay modules, buttons, potentiometers, LEDs — and the catalog grows with each update.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

We're in early access. Direct APK distribution lets us ship improvements faster. A Play Store release is planned.

How do I cancel Pro?

Email us any time and we'll cancel immediately — no lock-in.

Safety note: wiring suggestions are a starting point. Always double-check voltage levels and polarity before powering a circuit, and never work with mains voltage unless qualified.
